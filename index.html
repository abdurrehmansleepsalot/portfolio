About Me

I'm Abdur Rehman — a passionate developer and professional photographer who thrives at the intersection of technology and creativity. Whether I'm architecting efficient algorithms or composing the perfect shot, I bring the same meticulous attention to detail to everything I do.

I love the art of dry-running, debugging, and dissecting problems until they yield. There's something deeply satisfying about tracing a bug to its root or optimising a loop to run in half the time. I'm always learning — currently deepening my expertise in data structures, algorithms, and machine learning.

On the creative side, I'm a proficient photographer and video editor. I work with Adobe Lightroom, Adobe Photoshop, and DaVinci Resolve to produce visuals that tell compelling stories. My technical background makes my creative work precise, and my creative eye makes my technical work beautiful.
